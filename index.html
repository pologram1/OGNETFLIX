Add a movie

The rental journey.

  1. Discover. Open a movie and add it to your queue.
  2. Dispatch. Switch to Administrator, choose the member, and dispatch their next active title.
  3. Watch. Switch to Member and mark the movie as watched in At home.
  4. Return. An administrator registers the return. The next active queued movie dispatches automatically.

This is a local demo. The workspace switch demonstrates roles; it is not account authentication. No real rental, payment, or streaming service is connected.

The original Java/JSP application remains in the GitHub repository.
